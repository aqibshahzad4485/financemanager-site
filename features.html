Core Engine

Multi-Currency & Core Engine

Global Multi-Currency

Travel or invest globally? Finance Manager inherently supports multiple currencies, letting you track assets natively without manual conversions or inaccurate exchange rates.

Smart Inter-Currency Transfers

Moving money between international accounts is seamless. Built-in logic handles custom exchange rates and balance adjustments automatically across different currencies.

Loans & Debt Management

Don't lose track of borrowed or lent money. Maintain a dedicated ledger for loans given out or debts owed, complete with settlement history and repayment tracking.

Unlimited Ledger Entries

Log as many expenses, incomes, and transfers as you want without ever hitting a paywall, subscription, or forced upgrade limit.

Management

Accounts, Budgets & Structure

Wallets & Accounts

Manage diverse financial assets seamlessly. Track cash wallets, checking accounts, credit cards, and savings accounts in their respective currencies.

Smart Categories & Tags

Organize your spending with custom categories and tags. Classify transactions to see where your money flows every single month.

Advanced Budgeting

Set strict limits for individual categories or overall spending. Monitor your progress with real-time indicators to prevent overspending.

Infinite Granularity

Structure your finances your way. From crypto holdings to cash piggy banks, customize labels and structures to match your personal system.

Insights & Security

Analytics, Privacy & Controls

Visual Insights & Charts

Understand spending habits instantly. Generative charts and aesthetic analytics reveal breakdown by category, trend over time, and net balance changes.

Absolute Privacy & Offline

Your financial footprint is strictly yours. Finance Manager operates 100% offline with zero cloud lock-in, zero backend servers, and zero telemetry.

Database Backup & Export

Never lose your history. Backup and restore your raw database locally, or export your full transaction records into CSV and PDF reports.

Custom Alerts & Themes

Set custom notifications for budget thresholds and loan settlements. Seamlessly toggle between crafted Dark and Light visual themes.
